# Three Business Hub — Group Operating Plan

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This document is the shared operating plan for the three separate businesses. Keep each business's contacts, records, finances and permissions separate. Share a lead between businesses only where relevant and lawful, with appropriate permission.

## Group structure

- **Kiely Industrial Coatings (K.I.C.)** — industrial coatings, repainting and refurbishment projects.
- **CoatMatch UK** — specialist subcontractor discovery, verification and work-package introductions.
- **Pipeline Pilot AI UK** — prospect research and sales-pipeline support; exact product scope and pricing remain subject to validation.

## K.I.C. commercial strategy

- Target gross project margin: **25%**.
- Preferred delivery model: subcontract management and project coordination.
- Also assess self-delivery where hired equipment and competent labour produce better risk-adjusted profit.
- Do not assume K.I.C. owns plant, premises, vehicles or workforce.
- Hire equipment only after a viable job and safe delivery plan are established.
- Customer deposits and staged payments are preferred where negotiated, but never assumed, especially for public contracts.
- Required selling price for a 25% gross margin = total direct project cost / 0.75.

### K.I.C. markets to investigate

1. Skips, RoRo containers, waste bins and steel containers.
2. Shipping and storage containers.
3. Industrial cladding, roofs and cut-edge corrosion treatment.
4. Structural steel, bridges, railings and metalwork.
5. Tanks, silos and pipework.
6. Plant, machinery, trailers and agricultural equipment repainting.
7. Industrial floor coatings and concrete protection.
8. Shutters, gates, fencing, bollards and street furniture.
9. Warehouse, factory and industrial estate maintenance.
10. Renewable energy and utility infrastructure where competence and access requirements can be met.

### K.I.C. delivery and bid gates

Before any bid or commitment, check: exact scope/specification; site survey/access; coating system and surface preparation; labour and subcontractor quotations; materials; equipment/access hire; transport; waste/wastewater controls; insurance; competence/training; RAMS/COSHH; environmental controls; programme; payment terms; retention; bonds; working capital; contingency; and 25% margin.

Do not claim completed jobs, references, accreditations, insurance, approved teams or owned equipment unless verified. Do not start physical work until cover, competence, site controls and written customer terms are confirmed. Welding, structural repairs, lifting and safety-critical work require suitably competent specialists.

### Opportunity classification

- Open direct tender.
- Framework/DPS membership or call-off.
- Pre-market/future pipeline.
- Awarded contract / subcontract route.
- Private-sector prospect.

For every opportunity record official source, buyer, location, scope, value and what that value represents, current status, deadline, eligibility, portal, main contractor if awarded, likely K.I.C. package, next action, confidence and source-check date. Framework headline values are not K.I.C. revenue. Deduplicate opportunities and never pad the pipeline.

## CoatMatch UK

Build both sides of the network:
- Supply: specialist industrial painters, protective-coating applicators, roof/cladding teams, concrete repair specialists, steelwork and other relevant subcontractors.
- Demand: principal contractors, industrial maintenance firms, FM providers, asset owners, fabricators, waste/container operators and property managers.

Capture trade, geography, capacity, availability, evidence of experience, insurance and relevant competence documents, verification status, official contact route, work-package fit and introduction history. Never describe a contractor as vetted/insured/accredited until documentary checks are complete. Respect opt-outs and avoid duplicate outreach. Test qualified introductions before investing in expensive marketplace development.

## Pipeline Pilot AI UK

The precise product and capabilities are not yet confirmed. Validate customer demand before claiming features or selling software. Research possible offers such as prospect research, CRM/pipeline administration, lead qualification, sales follow-up preparation and AI workflow setup. Record buyer pain points, competing offers, evidence, pricing benchmarks and delivery effort. Do not promise lead volumes, conversion rates or revenue. Do not contact prospects until the offer is approved.

## Shared hub record structure

Maintain separate business records for:
- Sales pipeline / opportunities.
- CRM contacts and organisations.
- Contractor network and verification.
- Projects and jobs.
- Quotations and cost breakdowns.
- Tasks and follow-ups.
- Activity log and source evidence.

Suggested stages: Researching → Ready to contact → Contacted → Replied → Qualified → Quotation → Won/Lost. Drafts are not sent messages; sent does not mean delivered or read; no revenue is counted until evidenced.

## Group reporting

Weekly report should separate:
- New researched opportunities.
- Verified prospects.
- Drafts prepared.
- Messages actually sent.
- Replies, bounces and opt-outs.
- Qualified enquiries.
- Quotations.
- Won/lost work.
- Actual revenue and gross profit, when evidenced.
- Upcoming deadlines and owner decisions.

No automatic sending, tender submission, acceptance of work, purchases, financial commitments or account changes without the owner's explicit approval.
