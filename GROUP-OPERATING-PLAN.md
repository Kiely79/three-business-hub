# Three Business Hub — Group Operating Plan

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This is the controlling plan for the three businesses. **The hub is one joined-up group: all three businesses should actively help generate work, customers, supply, delivery capacity and revenue for one another wherever relevant, lawful and commercially sensible.** Keep each legal/business record, inbox, finances and customer permissions separate, but coordinate opportunities and referrals across the group. Do not make the owner repeat settled instructions.

## Group structure and how the businesses work together

- **Kiely Industrial Coatings (K.I.C.)** — industrial coatings, repainting and refurbishment. K.I.C. does straightforward, manageable hands-on work itself, including suitable skip/container jobs, and uses independent specialists for larger, complex, high-access or capacity-heavy work.
- **CoatMatch UK** — finds and connects buyers with specialist subcontractors, and finds work opportunities for trades. It can help K.I.C. source independent specialist capacity; K.I.C. may be a potential provider for suitable CoatMatch opportunities only where the scope, capacity, insurance and customer permission fit. Do not give K.I.C. preferential or false "vetted" status.
- **Pipeline Pilot AI UK** — prospect research, lead qualification and sales-pipeline support. It can support the group's own prospect research and pipeline administration as an internal proving ground, while separately developing a paid offer for external SMEs. Do not count internal group use as external revenue or claim unbuilt software features.
- Use the shared hub to coordinate cross-business opportunities. A lead may be relevant to more than one business, but create linked, clearly labelled records in each business pipeline rather than merging their contacts, consent, contracts or finances. Record the relationship and reason for the cross-referral.
- Cross-sell naturally: a K.I.C. buyer may need other verified trades via CoatMatch; CoatMatch buyers/trades may need K.I.C.'s coating services; Pipeline Pilot may help either business organise sales leads, but external customers must be separately qualified and priced. Never force an irrelevant referral or share personal/contact data without a lawful basis and permission.

## K.I.C. commercial strategy

- K.I.C. is a hybrid hands-on and subcontracted delivery business, not solely a managing agent.
- K.I.C. directly undertakes straightforward coating and repainting work it is competent and properly equipped to perform, especially suitable skips, RoRo containers, bins and other accessible/easy steel items.
- Larger, specialist, complex, high-access, safety-critical or capacity-heavy jobs can be priced and delivered with independent subcontractors. Do not assume every job is subcontracted or every job self-delivered.
- K.I.C. manages enquiries, customer relationships, surveys/briefing as agreed, specifications, quotations, contracts, work planning, quality evidence and handover. For self-delivered jobs, K.I.C. also carries out the physical application work.
- Do not claim employees, owned plant, premises, vehicles, accreditations, references or completed projects unless verified.
- Owner's cash-flow preference: do not purchase or activate insurance prematurely before there is a realistic job/revenue opportunity and the cost is justified. Obtain and compare quotations without accepting or paying for a policy prematurely.
- **Start-work gate:** this cash-flow preference does not authorise uninsured work. Before accepting or commencing a job, check contractual requirements and applicable legal duties, and put appropriate insurance in force for the actual activities and delivery model. If cover is not active, do not start physical work; seek customer-funded mobilisation/deposit where appropriate or a properly insured subcontract route. Never tell a buyer K.I.C. is insured until evidence confirms it.
- For subcontracted work, verify the subcontractor's relevant insurance, competence, RAMS, COSHH and access controls; contracts must allocate responsibilities and insurance requirements correctly.
- Prefer customer deposits/staged payments where negotiated, but never assume a public buyer will pay upfront.
- Target gross project margin: **25%**. Required selling price for a 25% gross margin = total direct project cost / 0.75.
- Quote easy skip/container jobs as clear packages. Include preparation, coating system, materials, labour, transport, access, waste controls, drying/curing, exclusions and payment terms. Do not promise price before scope and costs are known.

9. Warehouse, factory and industrial estate maintenance.
10. Renewable energy and utility infrastructure where competence/access requirements can be met.

### K.I.C. bid and delivery gates
Check scope/specification; survey/access; coating system and preparation; subcontractor quotes where relevant; materials; equipment hire; transport; waste/wastewater; insurance; competence/training; RAMS/COSHH; environmental controls; programme; payment terms; retention/bonds; working capital; contingency; and 25% margin. Do not start physical work until applicable insurance and safety requirements are satisfied, site controls agreed and written terms signed. Welding, structural repairs, lifting and safety-critical work require competent specialists.

## CoatMatch UK

Build both sides:
- **Supply:** specialist industrial painters, protective-coating applicators, roof/cladding teams, concrete repair specialists, steelwork and other relevant subcontractors.
- **Demand:** principal contractors, industrial maintenance firms, FM providers, asset owners, fabricators, waste/container operators and property managers with identifiable work packages.
- For each contractor capture trade, geography, capacity, availability, evidence, insurance, competence, verification status and permission to be introduced.
- For each buyer capture actual package, location, timing, buyer contact route, onboarding rules, required trade and next action.
- Never describe a contractor as vetted/insured/accredited until documents are checked. Obtain contractor permission before sharing details. Respect opt-outs.
- Where relevant, offer K.I.C. as a potential coating provider, subject to the same neutral verification, scope, capacity, insurance and commercial checks as every other contractor.

## Shared hub, cross-business workflow and daily follow-up

Maintain separate business records for sales opportunities, contacts, contractor network/verification, projects/jobs, quotations/costs, tasks, activity and evidence. Use linked records for cross-business opportunities.

Stages: Researching → Ready to contact → Contacted → Replied → Qualified → Quotation → Won/Lost. Draft is not sent; sent is not delivered/read; a lead is not a qualified enquiry; framework value is not business revenue.

**Daily review/chase routine (or sooner when a deadline/reply requires it):**
1. Review all three business pipelines and shared group opportunities.
2. Check due/overdue next actions, tender deadlines, supplier-registration windows, unanswered human replies and quotations awaiting a response.
3. Check all three inboxes for meaningful incoming messages, bounces, opt-outs, deadlines and enquiries. Monitor only; do not reply to incoming messages unless owner explicitly authorises a specific reply.
4. For every open record, decide whether to research, qualify, prepare a follow-up, wait, close as lost/not relevant, or escalate to owner. Do not send repeated chasers too frequently; respect the contact's stated timetable and prior correspondence.
5. Find genuinely fresh opportunities for all three businesses, deduplicate by organisation, buyer, tender/reference and email history, and record official source, source-check date, evidence, fit, next action and confidence.
6. Use Pipeline Pilot methods to improve group lead quality and follow-up; use CoatMatch to find matching trade capacity for K.I.C. and other buyers; use K.I.C. as a potential provider where appropriate.
7. Report urgent items first, then each business separately and any cross-business referrals. Distinguish actual completed checks/actions from recommended tasks. If no meaningful change, do not invent activity.
